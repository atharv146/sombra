Cal/OSHA §3395 (Heat Illness Prevention): The regulation mandates shade when temperatures exceed 80°F, requires employers to encourage drinking one quart of water per hour, initiates high-heat procedures at 95°F, and requires a 10-minute preventative cool-down rest period every two hours for agricultural workers.
Link: https://www.dir.ca.gov/title8/3395.html

Cal/OSHA §5141.1 (Protection from Wildfire Smoke): These rules apply to workplaces where the current AQI for PM2.5 is 151 or greater, requiring employers to provide respirators for voluntary use. At an AQI of 500 or greater,respirator use is generally mandatory.
Link: https://www.dir.ca.gov/title8/5141_1.html

California Labor Code 226.2: This labor code mandates that employees compensated on a piece-rate basis must be paid separately for their authorized rest and recovery periods.
Link: https://www.dir.ca.gov/pieceratebackpayelection/AB_1513_FAQs.htm

CDC MMWR 2008 (Farmworker Heat-Death Rate): The CDC's June 20, 2008 report confirms that the average annual heat-related death rate for crop workers was 0.39 deaths per 100,000 workers, which is nearly 20 times greater than the 0.02 rate for all U.S. civilian workers.
Link: https://www.cdc.gov/mmwr/preview/mmwrhtml/mm5724a1.htm

San Joaquin County Farmworkers: Agriculture supports approximately 35,000 to 36,000 jobs in San Joaquin County.In the agricultural eastern region of the county (Lodi, Ripon, and Escalon), Spanish is the primary household language for 44,053 households, and Punjabi is the primary language for 1,575 households.
